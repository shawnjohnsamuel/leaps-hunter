# 🌎 DAILY MACRO REGIME ANALYSIS (MANDATORY)

Before evaluating ANY trade, determine the current macro regime and how it affects asymmetric LEAPS.

Collect and summarize:

## Interest Rates + Liquidity

* Current:

  * US 2Y Treasury yield
  * US 10Y Treasury yield
  * US 30Y Treasury yield
* 1-month and 3-month trend direction
* Yield curve status:

  * Steepening?
  * Inverted?
  * Disinverting?
* Fed Funds Rate
* Latest Fed commentary / FOMC tone
* Probability of next rate cut/hike pause
* QT/QE/liquidity conditions
* Credit spreads (HY spreads preferred)

### Interpretation Rules

* Rising long-end yields:

  * Negative for long-duration growth
  * Positive for financials/insurance/value
* Falling yields:

  * Bullish for software, AI, semis, duration assets
* Credit stress widening:

  * Reduce appetite for speculative unprofitable names
* Liquidity expansion:

  * Increases probability of narrative expansion

* Entire thesis depends on one product approval = REJECT

* Bid/ask spread >5% = REJECT

Reject trades that fundamentally conflict with current macro regime:

* Long-duration speculative tech during sharply rising yields
* Cyclicals during clear recession deterioration
* Financials during aggressive credit deterioration
* Small-cap leverage plays during tightening liquidity

* Catalysts beyond option duration = REJECT
* Theta decay consumes >70% of expected gain = REJECT
